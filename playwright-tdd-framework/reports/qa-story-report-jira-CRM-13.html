Executive Summary

Scope: Login form validation only

CRM-13 defines four validation outcomes on the login page: invalid email format or domain name, email length above 400 characters, password length above 100 characters, and a general invalid credential message after a failed login attempt. This is a UI-centric story with a small technical surface, so the main QA goal is to confirm field-level feedback, message placement, and exact copy under both client-side and submit-time failure paths.

Overall assessment: the story is testable as written, but it leaves room for interpretation around empty-field behavior, valid credential success flow, and whether validation happens instantly or only after submit.

Requirement Breakdown

Decomposed from story text

Functional requirements

  • The Business Email field must reject invalid email formats and invalid domain names.
  • The Business Email field must reject values longer than 400 characters.
  • The Password field must reject values longer than 100 characters.
  • The login action must show a bottom-of-page error message for incorrect credentials.

Validation and UX rules

  • Invalid email and long email inputs must highlight the Business Email field in red.
  • Long password input must highlight the Password field in red.
  • Error copy must match the story exactly where specified.
  • Errors should be easy to discover and should not require page reload to interpret.

Non-functional expectations

  • Validation should behave consistently across supported browsers.
  • Error messages should remain readable and not clip at common desktop viewports.
  • Login failure must not expose sensitive credential details.

Gaps / open questions

  • No explicit acceptance criteria for empty Business Email or Password fields.
  • No explicit success-path criteria for valid credentials.
  • No stated rule for when validation should appear: on blur, on typing, or after submit.
  • No accessibility rule for screen reader announcements or focus movement.
