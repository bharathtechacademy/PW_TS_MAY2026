Planning Guidance

  • Prioritize the page-load banner, exact copy, and close-on-action flows in the regression pack because they are the highest-risk release checks.
  • Automate the stable UI contracts first: header text, body text, control presence, and consent button actions.
  • Keep viewport, keyboard, and cross-browser checks in the functional slice unless they become release blockers or are standardized across the product.
  • Treat the consent persistence refresh check as an exploratory follow-up until the story clarifies the expected retention rule.
